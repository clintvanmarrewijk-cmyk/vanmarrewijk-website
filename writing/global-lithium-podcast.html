Global Lithium Podcast — Episode 187: Zelandez

with Joe Lowry · May 2024 · 51 min

Clint joins Joe Lowry on the Global Lithium Podcast to talk about what Zelandez actually does in the field — from drilling supervision and logging to hydrogeology and brinefield management across South America. The conversation covers the realities of lithium brine exploration that don't make it into press releases: completion errors, the DLE hype cycle, and why most explorers underestimate reinjection costs.

Joe and Clint also discuss the first wave of explorers leaving the lithium space, the second wave of capital coming in without the hard-won experience of the first cycle, and why the industry's biggest challenge isn't supply — it's knowledge transfer.
