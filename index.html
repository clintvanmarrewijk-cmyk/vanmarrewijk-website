Expertise

What I know about

⚖

Brinefield Management

Full operational management for explorers — from drilling supervision through resource estimation to PEA. We provide the announcement timetable that keeps junior explorers in conversation with the market.

⚙

DLE Advisory

Independent guidance on Direct Lithium Extraction technology selection. The hard parts aren't the DLE itself — they're pretreatment, reinjection, and designing the full flowsheet before choosing a vendor.

☍

Resource Estimation

NI 43-101 and JORC-compliant resource estimates built on proper subsurface characterization. We've seen what happens when you drill shallow first — the resource always changes.

🔍

Technical Due Diligence

Independent technical reviews for M&A, financing, and investment decisions. We assess chapter maturity relative to project stage — working backwards from what the FID data room needs.

🌐

Cross-Boundary Dynamics

Brine doesn't respect property boundaries. Pumping from one well changes reservoir pressure for miles. The lithium industry hasn't adopted unitization frameworks yet, but it will have to.

♼

Reinjection

Mandatory in many jurisdictions, often 10-20% of total project economics, and frequently underestimated. We model reinjection requirements before they become surprises.

📈

Capital Raising & Company Building

Self-funded SaferMe from startup to operating in 15+ countries. Raised capital across multiple structures — SAFEs, equity rounds, and strategic partnerships. Built and led teams through product-market fit and international expansion.

💰

Investment Strategy

Four years at Gareth Morgan Investments covering product strategy, portfolio administration, and quantitative security analysis. Built financial models, launched KiwiSaver products, and delivered GIPS-compliant performance reporting.

💻

Software & SaaS Products

Co-founded SaferMe (formerly ThunderMaps) — a SaaS safety and hazard reporting platform. End-to-end product leadership from concept through to enterprise sales across government, utilities, and infrastructure sectors.

🛠

Mechanical Engineering

BE (Hons) in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Canterbury. Specialised in fluid dynamics, thermodynamics, and energy engineering — the foundation for understanding subsurface brine behaviour.
